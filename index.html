Machine stopped? Try a quick fix

Answer a few taps and we will walk you through the simple checks that get most small problems moving again. Built for use on your phone, right next to the machine.

Stop and call us straight away if you see any of these

  • Smoke, sparks or a burning smell
  • Fuel or hydraulic oil leaking or spraying
  • Brakes that feel soft, or steering that fails
  • A bent mast, loose chain or damaged forks
  • A battery that is hot, swollen or leaking
  • Exposed wiring, sparking or an electric shock
  • Generator exhaust fumes in a closed space
  • Any fault with a crane’s lifting or safety devices

Switch off, keep people away from the machine and call. Do not try to repair these yourself.

Questions we hear often

Do you service machines that you did not sell?

Yes. Our engineers work on forklifts, reach trucks, stackers, generators, cranes, tractors and EV equipment across brands, not only the ones we supply.

What do I need to tell you to get a quote?

The machine type, make and model, load capacity, where it will work, and your city. For parts, add the serial number and a photo if possible.

What is the difference between AMC and CMC?

An AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) covers scheduled servicing and inspections for your machines. A CMC (Comprehensive Maintenance Contract) goes further and also covers spare parts and breakdown repairs as set out in the contract.

How quickly can you respond to a breakdown?

Tell us your location and the fault. We send an engineer as soon as possible.

Are the spare parts genuine?

We supply genuine spare parts for the equipment we support.

Can you look after EV forklift batteries?

Yes. We service battery-operated equipment, including battery checks and charging system support.

Do you rent out equipment?

Yes. We rent out battery and diesel forklifts, stackers, reach trucks and generators for short and long periods. Tell us the equipment, capacity and how long you need it.

Do you sell and service generators?

Yes. We supply generators, spare parts for them, and service and repair them, including scheduled maintenance.
